# SNAKE'D — Rules

## Setup
- 2–6 players. The deck depends on player count (five suits: red, blue, green, pink, black):

  | Players | Cards used | Deck | Buried |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 2 | 2–6 | 25 | 7 |
  | 3 | 1–7 | 35 | 8 |
  | 4 | 0–8 | 45 | 9 |
  | 5 | 0–10 | 55 | 10 |
  | 6 | 0–10 | 55 | 1 |

- Deal 9 cards each; leftover cards are buried face down.
- Everyone starts with 1 frog.
- Reveal: three times, every player secretly picks a card from their hand, then all flip face up at the same moment. After three reveals everyone shows 3 cards.

## Trump and first lead
- Trump is the colour with the most face-up cards. Black (snakes) can never be trump.
- Tie: the tied colour with the highest single face-up card wins. Still tied: no trump this round.
- First lead: highest face-up snake. If no snakes are showing, the highest face-up card of any colour. Still tied: random.

## Tricks (8 per round)
- You must follow the led colour if you can, using face-up or hidden cards.
- Highest trump wins; otherwise the highest card of the led colour wins.
- If any snake is played in a trick, the player of the highest snake leads the next trick, even if they didn't win it. Otherwise the trick winner leads.
- You must always have at least one card face up. If you play your last face-up card, immediately turn up a hidden card of your choice.
- The round ends with each player holding one face-up card: their prediction.

## Scoring
- Each trick won: 1 dragonfly = 1 point.
- Win exactly as many tricks as your last card's number:
  - Coloured card: take 1 frog (3 points).
  - Snake: steal 1 frog from an opponent instead (no new frog). If no opponent has a frog, take one from the supply.
- 3 rounds. Most points wins; ties go to the player with the most frogs.
